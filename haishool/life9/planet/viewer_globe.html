Planet and legend

Planet

The habitat globe is the planet's pattern in miniature. Its surface, gravity, air, light, temperature and chemistry are the planet's own derived values; its geography keeps the planet's ocean coverage, latitude and substellar climate and crust composition; curvature, the horizon and walking distances are those of the small globe (PLANET-SPEC section 1, labelled new_rule).

Surface

  • Ocean at sea level , darker where deeper
  • Bare ground, dry to wet soil
  • Plants: green deepens with plant carbon
  • Snow on land, ice on the sea
  • Land before the first field frame: low to high ground

Relief is drawn ×1.

Star light

The globe is lit from the star's direction. The line around the globe is the terminator, where the star sits on the horizon.

Calls

No meaning is supplied. A call is an 8-number vector the brain emits. A ring is drawn when loudness is at least the chosen level; its size follows loudness and its colour is only the index of the vector's largest component. Nothing in the world gives a call, a channel or an action a meaning or a reward.
