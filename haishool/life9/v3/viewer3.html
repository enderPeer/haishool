Legend and notes

Nothing is pre-installed. The bodies begin with random brains and random body genes. Nothing tells them what to eat or drink, where to go, what a sound means or what is good; no score is handed out, and they have no built-in wiring and no named purposeful moves. Supplied are only physics and chemistry, the planet's chain, replication with heritable random variation, death by physics, and a body's physical abilities.

Colours are display only. The colours of lineages and item classes (and of call channels) are chosen by this viewer. The bodies do not know them: their senses carry only physical channels such as light, sound, smell and touch.

Planet

Globe

  • Ocean below the sea level, darker where deeper
  • Land, low to high ground
  • Ice (recorded per cell)
  • Patch marker (not to scale): click it to show the patch. Zoom in with "Go to patch" to see the patch's true-size outline.

Temperature overlay (recorded cell air temperature):

–273 K (0 °C)–

The globe is lit from the star's direction in each frame. The line is the terminator, where the star sits on the horizon.

Patch ground

  • Bare ground (slightly lighter on higher ground)
  • Plants: green deepens with the recorded plant density (0–255)
  • Standing water: blue deepens with the recorded water (0–255)
  • Lake basins from the terrain, shown while no water is recorded
  • Sea: cells below the planet's sea level
  • Patch edge. The boundary is periodic: leaving one side enters the opposite side.

Heights are drawn at true scale (×1).

Bodies: lineage

  • A body: an ellipsoid of its volume, pointing along its heading
  • Dark dot at the front: the mouth output, larger when more open
  • Faint streak: the thrust output, behind for forward and ahead for backward thrust

Size follows mass: each body is drawn as an ellipsoid of its true volume at 1,050 kg/m³, enlarged by the factor shown on the patch view so it can be seen. While enlarged, no body is drawn smaller than 1.2 or larger than 12 pixels across its radius (items: 7), so a few large ones cannot hide the rest; "True size" draws everything at its real size. Click a body for its numbers.

Calls

No meaning is supplied. A call is an 8-number vector the brain emits. A ring is drawn when loudness is at least the chosen level; its size follows loudness and its colour is only the index of the vector's largest component.

Items and fires

  • Fire: a glow, deep red to yellow-white as it gets hotter

Items are drawn as small diamonds of the volume of their mass at 2,000 kg/m³ (their make-up is not in the record), enlarged like the bodies.
