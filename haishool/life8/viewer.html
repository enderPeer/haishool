This is a bounded model of individual behaviour in a two-dimensional ecology. Markers show recorded positions and energy, not a reconstruction of atomic anatomy. Signals are arbitrary symbols; their presence alone does not establish language. Useful tools, cultural transfer and cooperation require causal tests. Time is measured in simulation ticks, not historical years.
