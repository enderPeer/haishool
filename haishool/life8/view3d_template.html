Legend

A 2D world, shown in 3D. The world is a flat plane whose edges wrap around. It is drawn in perspective and heights are only there to make things visible: nothing in this world has a height. A real 3D world is the next round.

Inhabitants

  • A body; its size follows body mass (radius ∝ √mass). The small cone points where it last moved.
  • Ring on the ground: outer arc energy (share of 60), inner arc health; red is health lost for good to scars.
  • Visitor: gold halo and a label. The field is reserved for a later round; nothing sets it yet.

Colour: founder lineage

Each colour is one founder's descendants (the eight lineages with the most individual-frames); grey is every other lineage. Select a lineage to highlight it.
