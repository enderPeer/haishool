Open-source experiment · runs locally in Berlin

Haishool learns facts,
not language.

A small model trained from scratch only on dense facts: everyday objects, people, countries, religions, history, markets, science, software and pop culture. It never sees an English sentence; fixed rules turn its answers into English, and you can always see what the model itself said.

2 · Rules parseq japan capital. a
3 · Model answerstokyo
4 · Rules translateThe capital of Japan is Tokyo.
5 · You checkthe grey line shows the raw model output
