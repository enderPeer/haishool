Haishool round 5 · built and checked · training in progress

A universe small enough to check.

Facts and views come from people. Round 5 adds a second kind of truth: what follows from rules and can be checked by calculation. Maths, the elements, what things are made of, balanced reactions, the forces, and a toy universe that runs from the first second to molecules that copy themselves. Every answer has a gate: code that knows the truth and judges what the model says.

Six levels, each feeding the next

1 · First minutesProtons and neutrons become hydrogen and helium as the universe cools.
2 · CloudA rotating gas cloud collapses under its own gravity into a disc and clumps.
3 · PlanetsThe disc grows planets: rock inside the frost line, ice and gas outside.
4 · ChemistryAtoms bond by valence rules into water, methane, salt, rock.
5 · ReplicatorsMolecules that copy themselves, with mutation and selection.
6 · WorldThe chain as one timeline; each level hands its numbers to the next.

These are toy simulations with real rules, strongly simplified. They are not a simulation of the real universe, and nobody knows yet how life began. What they give is a world where every statement can be recomputed: mass, charge and atoms are conserved at every level, and a run is the same every time for the same seed.

Numbers are written digit by digit so the model can learn to calculate. Molar masses, mass percents and balances are computed from the formula, never typed in. Three independent checkers went through each table before it was accepted.

Can a small model calculate?

Yes, if it is fed differently. Same model, same size; only the diet changed. Questions the model never saw:

Maths questions never seen9,000 lines, each repeated 200 times1.5 million lines, each seen 11 times

Multiplication with the working written out98 to 100 %
Multiplication, bare answer56 %

A small file repeated often is learned by heart. An endless stream of fresh, checked examples is learned as a rule. So round 5 trains on a stream from the gates, writes its working out step by step, and lets the gate check every step.

Where it stands

  • doneFive gates, the feedback loop and six simulation levels are built; about 900 automated tests pass.
  • donePilot training: old facts and link paths are unharmed; lookups in the new tables are right for 80 to 95 % of unseen questions.
  • doneExperiment: the model learns to calculate from a stream of examples (table above).
  • nowAll parts are being merged into one dataset; calculations are being rewritten as worked steps.
  • nextFull training, then the feedback loop: the model answers fresh questions, the gates mark them right or wrong, and both go back into training.
  • openPhysics and chemistry formulas were 0 to 7 % right on unseen questions in the pilot. Simulation questions do not yet carry their inputs, so the model can only memorise the worlds it saw.

The model on the chat is still version 4b. Round 5 code and data will be published in the repository when the full training has run.
