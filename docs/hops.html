Haishool version 4b · Hops · runs locally in Berlin

Everything is linked
to everything.

Until now Haishool knew its facts as separate cards. Version 4b links all 3,081 of them into one graph: Turkey reaches Led Zeppelin, a spoon reaches the Beatles, in a few hops. Where people disagree, the link says who holds it. The model writes every path itself, and every step is checked against the real links.

A model is a mirror of the culture that describes the world. Contested things are not flattened into one "truth": the model learns q jerusalem capital_of. a israel palestine contested. and, for each side, what that side holds.

The links come from four places: 122 written by hand with sources and views; 5,075 where a record names another record (spoon made_of metal); shared values such as europe, 1970s or kitchen; and a few rules (located_in, shares_food) that follow from the others. The model also learns to say no: q turkey borders colombia. a no.

What it gets wrong

  • Near misses. Its "no" examples used far-away partners, so it says yes to "does Turkey border Israel?". The chat marks this wrong against the data.
  • True but pointless paths. Every link comes from the teacher's records, unchecked by a person; "spoon → ring → Nuremberg Trials" is made of real links built on a wrong record.
  • Only what is in its graph. It cannot link things it has never seen, and it does not chat.
